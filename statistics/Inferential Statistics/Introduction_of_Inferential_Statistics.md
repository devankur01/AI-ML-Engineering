### Descriptive vs Inferential  

**Descriptive Statistics**  

Descriptive Statistics only given data summarizes.  

Example 

We have 1000 Students marks.    

We can calculates the,  

- Mean
- Median    
- Mode  
- SD(Standard Deviation)    
- Histogram 

Only.   

We can only existing data describes.    

that's why their name is **Descriptive Statistics** 

**Inferential Statistics**  

Inferential Statistcs cannot do only existing data summarize.   

they can help of their data 
- Prediction.   
- estimation.   
- decision making.  
- Guess the future.     

Example 

We can only see the 1000 students marks 

predicts the "Entire collage average around 72."    

this is prediction, inference that's why we call it Inferential Statistics. 

### Defination  

Inferential Statistics is a branch of statistics that uses sample data to make conclusion or predicitons about the entire population.   

"In Simple Words, We can use the small data(sample data) to takes decision of their big data."  

that is Inferential Statistics. 

Meaning of **Population** and **Sample**.   

**Population means complete data.** 

**Sample means Population small representative part.**
